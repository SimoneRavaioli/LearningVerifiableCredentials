## Target and design
LVC-3 is selected because credentials are learner-held and presented by the holder. LVC-1 and LVC-2 are not wallet maturity steps and are out of scope. W3C VC/VP and did:key Ed25519 are a documented implementation route. Begin with LearnCard's documented custom plugin composition, loading the shipped WASM locally, avoiding paid LearnCloud, Network and remote-context dependencies. Verify signed output with an independent maintained implementation; do not call LearnCard from the independent verifier.

Issuer and learner have distinct random keys. University issuer, holder, and employer have distinct authenticated roles. A local development vault is encrypted at rest; learner key custody in this single-machine demonstration is explicitly custodial. Published status lists and an issuer registry supply verifier snapshots, separate from learner records. Historical authorization intervals are retained. Recognition is an employer policy decision separate from signature/status/trust.

## LearnCard findings verified from published source
@learncard/core 9.4.38 exposes generateLearnCard/addPlugin. @learncard/didkit-plugin 1.10.3 provides local WASM, Ed25519 generation, DID resolution and credential/presentation signing. @learncard/didkey-plugin 1.1.38 derives did:key identities from random 32-byte seeds. @learncard/vc-plugin 1.6.4 exposes issueCredential and issuePresentation with proofFormat options. JWT and JSON-LD are supported in source; actual outputs must be probed before selecting securing mechanism. Existing README examples have stale init imports and Ceramic descriptions; current source and repository docs are preferred.

@learncard/sd-jwt-vc-plugin 0.2.16 supports SD-JWT receipt/verification; README's presentation roadmap differs from included present.ts. Do not infer working selective disclosure without executed tests. @learncard/holder-continuity 0.2.21 exports encrypted ZIP bundles and restores original identity; documents state that no third-party wallet directly imports its bundle today. Our local storage requires an adapter or a clearly documented separate backup format. Open Badges 3.0 and CLR 2.0 are reference models, not unconditional mandates to implement both. OpenID4VCI/VP are listed options; Phase 2 does not define a compulsory exchange profile.

## Priorities and honest gaps

C development: local encrypted storage, consent/role enforcement, published status cache, governed demonstration trust registry, independent verifier. D unresolved: national governance/accreditation, decades-long key archival, third-party wallet import, final protocol conformance, selective disclosure until tested, detailed Phase 2 schemas.
